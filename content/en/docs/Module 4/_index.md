The motto of this module:

{{< callout type="tip" >}}
 *"Price is what you pay. Value is what you get"* – Warren Buffett
{{< /callout >}}

The aim is to teach you how to find the intrinsic value of those companies that have caught your interest and passed the initial quick analysis.

## List of questions

In all the sections that follow you will find a category called *"List of questions".* It is worth noting that these lists are a general foundation and do not cover every specific case in each category. Their purpose is to serve as a guide and to point you in the right direction.

Some companies will not be able to answer certain questions, either because they do not operate in that way or because they are too young to provide, for example, a detailed historical track record.
