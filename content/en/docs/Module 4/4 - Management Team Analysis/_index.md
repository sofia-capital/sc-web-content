Not every possible case will be covered: some questions, such as capital management, news search or insider search, you will need to work on independently.

## List of questions

### Management team

- Who are they? How many years have they been at the company?
- Current capital management (we covered this in Capital Management Analysis)
- Meeting targets: we look for the team to be conservative in their estimates; if they miss, the market will punish them.
- Salaries, stock-based pay and incentives
- How many shares in the company do they own?
- Have they sold or bought shares recently?
- Look for any news related to the management team

### Insiders

People who are directly linked to the company and are part of the management team or have access to inside information.

- Who are they?
- Do they have power or influence over the management team? Do they hold any role in the company?
- How many shares in the company do they own? Have they sold or bought shares recently?
