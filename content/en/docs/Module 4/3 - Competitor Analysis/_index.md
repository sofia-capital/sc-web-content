Not every possible case will be covered: some questions, such as those on competitive positions, main competitor metrics, estimates or percentages, you will need to work on independently.

## List of questions

### Competitors

- **Identify the main competitors**

- **Competitive positions**
  - Which company is the sector leader?
  - Trend / leadership?

- **Competitor threats: is there a possibility of sector disruption?**

- **Calculate main competitor metrics** (revenue, margins, profit, ROIC/ROE, debt ratio, market share)

- **Calculate main sector and competitor valuation multiples**

### Company competitive advantage

{{< callout type="info" >}}
 The term "competitive advantage" is also known as MOAT
{{< /callout >}}

- **Does the company have any special intangible asset?**
  - Brand power, patent or regulatory license

- **Market power** (oligopoly, duopoly)

- **Switching costs for the company's products/services?**

- **Does the company have a network effect?**

- **Is the company the lowest-cost producer/operator?**

> **NOTE:** The cheapest company is not always the best investment.
