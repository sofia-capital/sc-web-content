This module covers two decisive pillars for the investor: **portfolio management** and **psychology in investing**. Without a solid foundation in both, fundamental analysis knowledge remains limited in practice.

- **Portfolio management**: construction, liquidity and the life cycle of positions (buying, holding and selling).
- **Psychology in investing**: behavioural rules that support decision-making.
