## Lending your stocks to IBKR

IBKR offers the **"Stock Yield Enhancement Program"**, which allows you to lend your stocks in exchange for a portion of the profits generated.

### Requirements

- Be an eligible client, approved for a margin account or have a cash account with equity greater than **$50,000 USD** (or equivalent).

### Example

If you own 5,000 shares of XYZ, valued at $375,000 USD, and the market rate is 9%, IBKR will pay you interest on the collateral (U.S. Treasury securities or cash), for example:

**$375,000 USD × 4.5% = $16,875 annually**
