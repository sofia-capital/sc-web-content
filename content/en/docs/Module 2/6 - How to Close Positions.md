Here we have two options: 

- **Sell a position:** allows you to select the amount of the position you want to liquidate.
- **Close a position:** automatically generates a sell order for the total position.

Click on the **three vertical dots** (from **Portfolio -> Positions**), you will see the options to sell or close:

![IBKR Close or Sell](/images/EN/M2/D6/EN-M2-D6-Sell-or-close.png)

1. Click on **Sell**, select the number of shares you want to sell. Here you are generating an order, so you have the same types: limit, market, midprice, etc. In this example the order is a limit type.
2. Then click on **"Sell Order"**.

![IBKR Sell Order](/images/EN/M2/D6/EN-M2-D6-Sell-order-ticket.png)

You will see the following, now you are waiting for someone willing to buy at $189.44:

![IBKR Sell Order part 2](/images/EN/M2/D6/EN-M2-D6-Sell-order-ticket-part2.png)

Once the sell order is completed, you will see the following in **Trading -> Orders and Transactions -> Trades**.

> **Note:** Only for this example, have changed the limit order to 189.29$, to find quicker a buyer

![IBKR Trading sell order](/images/EN/M2/D6/EN-M2-D6-Sell-order-ticket-negotiation.png)
