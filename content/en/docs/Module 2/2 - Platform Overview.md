## IBKR Description – Demo Account

**Link to the European page:**

- https://www.interactivebrokers.ie/en/home.php

When accessing the page, you will see two options:

![IBKR Option to open account](/images/EN/M2/D2/EN-M2-D2-IBKR-Demo-account-Real-account.png)

- **Open a real account**: requires identity document.
- **Free trial:** allows you to open a demo account to familiarize yourself with the platform.

### Login Link - Simulated Account

- https://www.interactivebrokers.ie/sso/Login

> **Note:** Remember to select the demo account option and enter your credentials.

![IBKR Login to simulated account](/images/EN/M2/D2/EN-M2-D2-IBKR-Login-Demo-account.png)

### Once inside, you will find:

![IBKR Main page](/images/EN/M2/D2/EN-M2-D2-IBKR-Main-dashboard.png)


1. **Informative message** indicating that it is a demo account and the money is not real.

2. **Account Section**, with relevant information:

   - **Settled cash:** Money available to trade.
   - **Unrealized P&L:** Floating gains or losses (Open positions).
   - **Realized P&L:** Gains or losses from closed positions.
   - **Maintenance margin:** Minimum required to maintain open positions (if you use leverage).
   - **Excess liquidity:** Money available relative to maintenance margin.
   - **Buying power:** Maximum available to open positions (includes leverage if enabled).
   - **Dividends:** Dividends received.

3. **Tabs on the right** (some parts are not translated to English):

   - **Dashboard:** General account summary.
   - **Positions:** All open positions.
   - **Performance:** Performance with charts and data.
   - **Balances:** Technical breakdown of balances, margins and cash.
   - **Portfolio News:** Relevant news from your portfolio companies.
   - **Impact Lens:** Assessment of the social/environmental impact of your investments.
